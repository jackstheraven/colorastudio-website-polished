Good to know

Questions,
answered.

Not sure what you need yet? A short conversation is enough to get started.

Can I request design and printing together?+

Yes. Share what you need and we can discuss the artwork, specifications, production, and delivery as one project.

How do I get an accurate quotation?+

Include the item, quantity, dimensions or sizes, preferred material or finish if known, branding artwork, reference images and your deadline. The more specific the brief, the more accurate the quote.

Can you handle custom-branded apparel?+

Yes. Send your preferred garment, quantity, sizes, branding artwork and deadline. We will confirm the most suitable branding method and provide a quotation.

Do you work with businesses and organizations of different sizes?+

Yes. We support businesses, startups, schools, events, organizations, institutions and teams with project-based creative and production needs.
